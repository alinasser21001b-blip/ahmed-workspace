# OSCE recall note — SAMPLE
Specialty: Pediatrics
Examiner: Dr. Ahmed
Year: 2026
Case: Nephrotic Syndrome

Q1: What vaccination issues matter in a child with nephrotic syndrome?
Expected: Avoid live vaccines while immunosuppressed; pneumococcal vaccination is important; fever is an emergency until infection is excluded.

Q2: What is the urine finding that supports nephrotic syndrome?
Expected: Heavy proteinuria with frothy urine; hematuria is not the dominant feature of typical minimal change disease.
