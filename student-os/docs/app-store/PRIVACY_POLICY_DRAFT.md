## What we collect

We collect only what is directly relevant to running Student OS:

- **Account information**: your email address and a hashed password. We never store your password in readable form.
- **Profile information**: your display name, handle, biography, academic affiliation (university, college, program, stage), and any topics of interest you declare.
- **Content you create**: posts, comments, questions, practice-quiz answers, and any files or photos you choose to upload.
- **Messages**: the content of direct and group messages you send through the app.
- **Interactions**: what you follow, block, mute, react to, save, and report.
- **Technical information**: your IP address and device/browser identification string, recorded when you sign in, for account-security purposes.
- **Usage information**: which features you use, recorded internally to operate and improve the product (for example, which learning topics you have practised).

We do **not** collect your location, your contacts, or any advertising identifier, and we do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. Nothing in Student OS shares your data with another company for advertising or any other purpose.

## Why we collect it

Every category above exists to run a specific feature you use directly — your account, your posts, your messages, your practice history. We do not collect data "just in case," and we do not build advertising profiles.

## Photo uploads — a specific note

When you upload a photo, we remove location and other identifying camera metadata (such as device model and timestamp) from the file automatically, on our server, before it is stored. We keep only what is needed to display the photo correctly, including its orientation. This happens for every upload — there is nothing to turn on or configure.

## How long we keep it

- Your account and everything in it is retained for as long as your account exists.
- If you delete your account, your profile, posts, comments, reactions, bookmarks, memberships, files, learning history and login sessions are permanently deleted.
- Messages you sent are handled specially: the message itself is removed, but the conversation record is preserved for the other participant, exactly as it would be if you had deleted the message yourself.
- Product-usage analytics may be retained after account deletion, but with your identity removed — they can no longer be linked back to you or your account.
- Session security records (IP address, device string) do not currently have a stated retention period; `EXTERNAL_OWNER_ACTION_REQUIRED: define and publish a retention period, or implement an automatic purge, before this is accurate.`

## How to delete your data

You can permanently delete your account, and everything described above, directly inside the app: **Settings → Delete account**. This requires your password and typed confirmation, and cannot be undone. No email or support request is needed.

## Your choices

- You can block another account at any time from their profile, and see and manage everyone you have blocked in Settings → Blocked accounts.
- You can report content, comments, messages, or profiles that violate our community standards directly from the app.
- You can request deletion of your account and data at any time (see above).

## Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard one-way hashing and are never visible to us in readable form.
